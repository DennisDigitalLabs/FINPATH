# Chapter One: Introduction

## 1.1 Background

Financial inclusion is an important component of economic development because access to appropriate financial services can improve individuals' ability to save, borrow, manage financial shocks, and participate in economic activity.

Young people in underserved communities may experience barriers to formal financial services. These barriers can include limited financial history, irregular income, limited access to appropriate credit products, and exposure to unexpected financial shocks.

FINPATH was developed to provide an explainable, machine-learning-based financial inclusion risk assessment and personalized decision-support system for underserved youth in Kenya.

## 1.2 Problem Statement

Traditional financial assessment approaches may provide limited insight into the factors contributing to an individual's financial inclusion risk. A system that combines machine learning with explainability can help identify risk patterns while providing understandable information that supports decision-making.

## 1.3 Main Objective

To develop an explainable machine-learning-based financial inclusion risk assessment and personalized decision-support system for underserved youth in Kenya.

## 1.4 Specific Objectives

1. To identify relevant factors associated with financial inclusion risk.
2. To preprocess and prepare financial inclusion data for machine-learning analysis.
3. To develop and evaluate machine-learning models for risk assessment.
4. To incorporate explainability into the prediction process.
5. To provide personalized recommendations based on assessment results.
6. To develop a secure web-based system for interacting with the prediction and recommendation services.

## 1.5 Significance

FINPATH is intended to support users by presenting risk information in an understandable manner and connecting assessment outcomes with personalized recommendations.

The system also provides a foundation for applying explainable machine learning to financial inclusion challenges affecting underserved youth.

## 1.6 Scope

The project covers data preparation, exploratory analysis, machine-learning modelling, model evaluation, explainability, backend API development, frontend development, database management, testing, and deployment preparation.

## 1.7 Organization of the Report

Chapter One introduces the project. Chapter Two reviews relevant literature. Chapter Three describes the methodology. Chapter Four presents the system design and implementation. Chapter Five presents testing and results. Chapter Six provides conclusions and recommendations.
