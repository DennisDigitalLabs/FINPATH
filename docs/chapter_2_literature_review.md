# Chapter Two: Literature Review

## 2.1 Introduction

This chapter reviews concepts and existing approaches related to financial inclusion, financial risk assessment, machine learning, explainable artificial intelligence, and personalized decision-support systems.

## 2.2 Financial Inclusion

Financial inclusion refers to the availability and effective use of appropriate financial services by individuals and businesses. Access to savings, credit, payments, insurance, and other financial services can contribute to economic participation and resilience.

## 2.3 Financial Inclusion Among Youth

Young people can face financial barriers caused by limited income, limited credit history, unemployment or underemployment, and insufficient access to suitable financial products.

These challenges create a need for approaches that can identify financial risk while also providing actionable guidance.

## 2.4 Machine Learning in Financial Risk Assessment

Machine-learning algorithms can identify relationships and patterns within financial and socioeconomic data. Classification algorithms such as logistic regression, random forests, and gradient-boosting methods can be applied to risk prediction problems.

## 2.5 Explainable Artificial Intelligence

Explainable artificial intelligence seeks to make machine-learning predictions understandable to users and stakeholders. Explainability is particularly important in financial applications because users should have meaningful information about the factors contributing to a prediction.

FINPATH incorporates model explainability to support transparency and interpretation of risk results.

## 2.6 Personalized Decision Support

A prediction alone may not provide sufficient value to an end user. Decision-support systems can translate analytical results into recommendations that are more actionable and understandable.

FINPATH therefore combines risk assessment with personalized recommendations.

## 2.7 Research Gap

Existing financial assessment approaches may focus primarily on prediction without adequately combining risk assessment, explainability, and personalized recommendations within a single system targeted at underserved youth in Kenya.

FINPATH addresses this gap by integrating these components into one web-based decision-support platform.

## 2.8 Conceptual Foundation

The system follows the general flow:

**User Information → Assessment → Data Processing → Machine-Learning Prediction → Explanation → Personalized Recommendation → Decision Support**

## 2.9 Summary

The literature reviewed establishes the relevance of financial inclusion, machine learning, explainability, and personalized decision support to the FINPATH problem domain.
